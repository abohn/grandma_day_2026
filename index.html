The Projects

Two Gifts, One Island

Both projects start with the same place — Grosse Ile — and end up as physical objects. One is the whole island from the air; the other is a close-up of the property itself.

Project 01

Grosse Ile from Above

A full 3D-printable map of the island — roads, buildings, greenspace, and the river — generated straight from OpenStreetMap data and sliced for the printer.

  • 1
    Export from OSM
    Draw a bounding box around Grosse Ile in OpenStreetMap, including building footprints, road network, and water boundaries.
  • 2
    Convert to 3D mesh
    Run through a map-to-STL tool (e.g. maps-for-3d-printing.com or osm2world). Scale to fit the build plate.
  • 3
    Slice & print
    0.15 mm layers, 15% infill, no supports. Print time ~6–9 hrs depending on scale.
Scale1 : 8,000 (fits ~200 mm plate)
MaterialPLA — light gray or white
Layer height0.15 mm
Infill15 % gyroid
SupportsNone needed
Project 02

The Three Buildings

A close-up miniature of the three structures on the property — captured with a phone, cleaned up, and printed at architectural model scale.

Recommended approach

Use KIRI Engine (free iOS/Android app) — walk slowly around each building once, the app photogrammetry-reconstructs a 3D mesh, and you export it as STL. Whole capture per building: ~10 minutes.

  • 1
    Capture each building
    Open KIRI Engine, shoot a slow orbit around the building. Bright overcast light is ideal. Upload and wait ~5 min for reconstruction.
  • 2
    Export & clean
    Download the STL. Open in Meshmixer (free) or PrusaSlicer — use "Make Solid" to close any holes and flatten the base.
  • 3
    Scale to match Project 01
    Match the scale of the map print so the buildings could theoretically sit on the island model. Or print at 1:100 for more detail.
  • 4
    Slice & print
    0.1 mm layers for more detail. Orient with the largest flat face down. Light supports for overhanging rooflines.
ToolKIRI Engine (free tier)
Scale1 : 100 for standalone display
MaterialPLA — cream or tan
Layer height0.10 – 0.15 mm
SupportsMinimal (touching buildplate only)

Print settings cheat sheet

These settings work on a Bambu Lab X1C, P1S, or Prusa MK4. Adjust for your machine — but keep layer height at 0.15 mm or lower for the map details to show up cleanly.

SlicerBambu Studio or PrusaSlicer (both free)
MaterialPLA, any brand. Matte finishes look more architectural.
Layer height0.15 mm map · 0.10 mm buildings
Infill15 % (map) · 10 % (buildings)
SupportsNone (map) · Touching buildplate only (buildings)
SpeedNormal / 50 % — slower = better surface detail

Notes & Tips

Things I Learned

Running notes on what worked, what didn't, and what I'd do differently. Updated as the project progresses.

On the map model

The island's long, narrow shape is almost perfectly sized for a standard 220 mm build plate when oriented vertically. The river shows up beautifully as a recessed flat area, and the green spaces (parks, yards) extrude just slightly above road level — really satisfying to look at.

On photogrammetry for buildings

KIRI Engine's free tier processes one model at a time and takes a few minutes per upload. Walk in a slow, overlapping spiral — don't skip the roof (get a ladder or tilt the phone up a lot). Flat surfaces like walls can confuse photogrammetry; adding a few reference points (tape an X of colored tape on the wall) helps the algorithm lock in.

Alternatives if KIRI Engine doesn't work out

Polycam — similar to KIRI, slightly better UI, free tier is limited.
Reality Scan (Epic) — free, good quality, requires account.
Tinkercad — skip photogrammetry entirely, hand-model rough box shapes. Less detail but totally printable and a fun afternoon project.
